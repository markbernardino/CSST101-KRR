Capstone title: AI-Powered Knowledge Representation and Reasoning System Using R

🚀 Project Requirements

Students design and implement a real-world intelligent system that explicitly represents knowledge and performs reasoning. R is the primary laboratory language and an approved LLM or local model may be used as an AI-assisted component.

RequirementMinimum Expectation
Knowledge RepresentationFacts, rules, predicates, semantic network, ontology, or knowledge graph.
ReasoningAt least one explicit mechanism: forward/backward chaining, argumentation, temporal/spatial reasoning, or planning.
R ImplementationWorking R code, functions, data structures, tests, and reproducible execution.
AI ComponentLLM, RAG, AI-assisted extraction, natural-language interface, or instructor-approved feature.
VerificationTest cases and comparison of AI output against symbolic/rule-based reasoning.
ExplainabilityFacts, rules, evidence, and reasoning supporting important outputs.
DocumentationTechnical report, diagrams, limitations, ethics, AI-use disclosure, and references.
PresentationWorking demonstration and oral defense.
