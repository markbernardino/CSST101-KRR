📄 Standard Case Study Output

  1. Problem statement and real-world context
  2. Dataset description and data dictionary
  3. R data-cleaning and exploratory analysis script
  4. At least two meaningful visualizations where applicable
  5. Knowledge representation
  6. Reasoning algorithm and R implementation
  7. AI prompt/output evidence when AI is used
  8. Verification/comparison between AI and R/KRR result
  9. Interpretation and recommendation
  10. Limitations, ethical considerations, and reflection
